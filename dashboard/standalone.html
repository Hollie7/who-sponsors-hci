Method and gaps口径与缺口

  • Data come from each edition’s sponsor page, sponsor prospectus and the SIGCHI conference history archive. Only logos whose organization could be identified are included; some names are inferred from image file names.
  • Gaps (CHI / CSCW / UIST / UbiComp): no lists for CHI 1999–2005 or CSCW before 2013 (early CSCW sites are gone). CHI 2012 Contributors appear only as an image and are not included. UbiComp 2020–2024 lists have no tiers, so no amounts are estimated. Local society and government “Supporters” are listed but not counted in amounts.
  • Country has two modes, switchable at the top. Headquarters uses the sponsoring entity’s own headquarters (Toyota Research Institute, FXPAL, MERL, Megagon Labs and Nokia Bell Labs are all in the US). Parent company uses the group parent (the first four count as Japan, Nokia Bell Labs as Finland). A few small companies could not be located. The countries tile uses headquarters.
  • Amounts are lower bounds: tier prices are floors, and themed sponsorships, booths and recruiting fees are ignored. Years without published prices reuse a neighboring year’s prices. Friend and Supporter tiers count as $1,000.
  • DIS, IUI and FAccT were added later. DIS lists exist only for 2016, 2019, 2022 and 2024–2026 (the 2014, 2017 and 2018 sites are gone or taken over; 2020–2021 were online with no sponsors shown), and only 2019 has prices. IUI covers 2015–2026 (missing 2013–2014) with prices every year; 2017 prices were in euros, converted at 1.13. FAccT covers 2018–2026; its site lists sponsors without tiers, so each is counted at the lowest tier that year ($5k before 2023, $10k from 2023), the lowest possible bound. About 40% of FAccT sponsors are foundations (MacArthur, Ford, Sloan, Luminate, Schmidt and others).
  • AIES (AAAI/ACM Conference on AI, Ethics, and Society) covers 2018–2026. Prices for 2023–2026 are Platinum $30k, Gold $20k, Silver $10k, Bronze $5k; 2020–2022 had tiers but no published prices, so the same prices are used; 2018–2019 had no tiers and are not estimated. The 2026 AI Policy Lab and Tech Policy Lab appear only as logos, so their institutions are inferred. Organizers AAAI, ACM and SIGAI are not counted.
  • Google DeepMind and DeepMind are counted as Google. FAccT and AIES often show DeepMind and Google logos side by side; these were entered as a single Google listing, so for those two conferences you cannot tell which editions DeepMind paid for.
  • Meta/Facebook/Oculus, Yahoo/Verizon Media/Oath, Tableau/Salesforce and similar are merged into one organization. Organizers such as ACM and SIGCHI are not counted as sponsors.
  • Flags come from the open-source flag-icons set; the Spanish flag omits the coat of arms.
  • 数据来自各届官网赞助页、招商书和 SIGCHI 历史存档，只收录网页上能识别出名字的 logo；部分 logo 只有文件名，机构名为推断。
  • 覆盖不全（CHI / CSCW / UIST / UbiComp）：CHI 1999–2005、CSCW 2012 年以前未取得名单（CSCW 早年官网已失效）；CHI 2012 的 Contributor 只以图片呈现，未收录；UbiComp 2020–2024 的名单没有标等级，所以无法估算金额；地方学会/政府“Supporter”计入名单但不计金额。
  • 国家/地区有两种口径，可在顶部切换：“总部所在地”按赞助实体自身的总部（Toyota Research Institute、FXPAL、MERL、Megagon Labs、Nokia Bell Labs 都在美国）；“母公司国籍”按集团母公司（前四家计为日本，Nokia Bell Labs 计为芬兰）。少数小公司无法确定所在地。顶部“涉及的国家/地区”卡片按总部口径计。
  • 金额是下限估算：等级价只是起步价，忽略主题赞助、展位、招聘等额外付费；未公布价格的年份沿用相邻年份价目；“Friend/Supporter”一律按 1,000 美元计。
  • DIS、IUI、FAccT 是后补的：DIS 只取得 2016、2019、2022、2024–2026 的名单（2014、2017、2018 的旧官网已失效或被占用，2020–2021 为线上会议、未见赞助），只有 2019 年有公开价目；IUI 取得 2015–2026（缺 2013–2014），每年都有公开价目，2017 年为欧元价、按 1.13 折算；FAccT 取得 2018–2026，官网只列名单不分等级，金额按当年最低一档（2022 年前 $5k，2023 年起 $10k）估算，所以是下限中的下限。FAccT 的赞助方约四成是基金会（MacArthur、Ford、Sloan、Luminate、Schmidt 等）。
  • AIES（AAAI/ACM AI、伦理与社会会议）取得 2018–2026 全部名单。2023–2026 年公开价目为 Platinum $30k / Gold $20k / Silver $10k / Bronze $5k；2020–2022 年有等级但没找到价目，沿用这一价目；2018–2019 年不分等级，不估算。2026 年的 AI Policy Lab 和 Tech Policy Lab 只有 logo，所属机构是推断的。主办方 AAAI、ACM、SIGAI 不计。
  • Google DeepMind、DeepMind 计入 Google。FAccT 和 AIES 的官网常把 DeepMind 和 Google 两个 logo 并列，录入时合并成了一条 Google，所以这两个会议里看不出哪一届是 DeepMind 出资。
  • Meta/Facebook/Oculus、Yahoo/Verizon Media/Oath、Tableau/Salesforce 等已合并为同一机构；ACM、SIGCHI 等主办方不计为赞助商。
