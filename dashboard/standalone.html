How sponsors are counted怎么计数

  • An organization counts once per conference edition, whatever its tier.同一机构在一届会议只计 1 次，不论等级。
  • Organizers are not sponsors: ACM, SIGCHI, SIGAI, SIGGRAPH, SIGMOBILE and AAAI are left out.主办方不算赞助商：ACM、SIGCHI、SIGAI、SIGGRAPH、SIGMOBILE、AAAI 都不计入。
  • Host universities and local government or society supporters are listed (tier “Supporter (host)” or “Special”) but not counted in amounts.承办高校和当地政府、学会的支持计入名单（等级为 “Supporter (host)” 或 “Special”），但不计金额。
  • Brands of one company are merged: Meta/Facebook/Oculus, Yahoo/Verizon Media/Oath, Tableau/Salesforce, and Google with DeepMind. FAccT and AIES often show Google and DeepMind side by side; these were entered as a single Google listing, so for those two conferences you cannot tell which editions DeepMind paid for.同一公司的不同品牌已合并：Meta/Facebook/Oculus、Yahoo/Verizon Media/Oath、Tableau/Salesforce，以及 Google 与 DeepMind。FAccT 和 AIES 常把 Google 和 DeepMind 两个 logo 并列，录入时合并成一条 Google，所以这两个会议看不出哪一届是 DeepMind 出资。

Estimated amounts估算金额

  • Every amount is a lower bound: each sponsor counts at the published floor price of its tier. Themed sponsorships, booths, recruiting and other extras are ignored.所有金额都是下限：每家赞助商按其等级的公开起步价计，忽略主题赞助、展位、招聘等额外付费。
  • Years without a published price list reuse the nearest year’s prices (the price basis column in the listings table says which). Friend and Supporter tiers count as $1,000.没有公开价目的年份沿用最近一年的价目（赞助条目表的“价格依据”一列有标注）。Friend 和 Supporter 等级按 $1,000 计。
  • An edition gets an estimate only when at least half of its paying sponsors have a known price; otherwise it is left out of the charts.只有一届中至少一半的出资赞助方有已知价格，才给出这一届的估算；否则图中不画。
  • FAccT is the weakest estimate: its tiers are unknown, so the lowest tier is assumed for every sponsor.FAccT 的估算最弱：等级未知，所有赞助方都按最低一档计。

Countries国家 / 地区

  • Two modes, switchable at the top. Headquarters uses the sponsoring entity’s own location: Toyota Research Institute, FXPAL, MERL, Megagon Labs and Nokia Bell Labs are in the US. Parent company uses the group’s home: Japan for the first four, Finland for Nokia Bell Labs, China for TikTok.两种口径，可在顶部切换。“总部所在地”按赞助实体自身所在地：Toyota Research Institute、FXPAL、MERL、Megagon Labs、Nokia Bell Labs 都在美国。“母公司国籍”按集团所在国：前四家计为日本，Nokia Bell Labs 计为芬兰，TikTok 计为中国。
  • A few small companies could not be located; they stay in the lists but get no country row. The countries tile at the top uses headquarters.少数小公司无法确定所在地，仍计入名单，但不单列国家。顶部“涉及的国家/地区”卡片按总部口径计。
