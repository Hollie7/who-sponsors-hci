Estimated sponsorship per edition每届估算赞助额

Each sponsor is counted at its tier’s published price and summed per edition. ACL, EMNLP and NAACL share one sponsorship program with booklets from 2019 on; 2018 reuses the 2019 prices, and earlier years are not estimated. Multi-conference packages are discounted, so a few sponsors paid less than the single-conference price.每家赞助商按其等级的公开价计，加总得到每届的估算。ACL、EMNLP、NAACL 共用一套赞助方案，2019 年起有招商手册；2018 年沿用 2019 年价格，更早的年份不估算。多会议打包有折扣，所以少数赞助商实际付款低于单会议价格。

How sponsors are counted怎么计数
  • An organization counts once per conference edition, at its highest tier. Diversity and inclusion sponsorships, local and destination partners (tourism boards, city governments), award and student-volunteer sponsors are listed but have no price, so they do not add to the estimates.同一机构在一届会议只计 1 次，取最高等级。多元与包容（D&I）赞助、本地和目的地合作方（旅游局、市政府）、奖项和学生志愿者赞助都列出，但没有价格，不计入估算。
  • Brands of one company are merged with the same rules as the ML page: DeepMind into Google; LinkedIn and Microsoft Translator into Microsoft; Toutiao into ByteDance; Naver Labs Europe into NAVER; Alibaba Cloud and Taobao into Alibaba (Ant Group kept separate); A9 into Amazon. HLTCOE and CLSP count as Johns Hopkins University, ISI as USC.同一公司的品牌按机器学习页面的规则合并：DeepMind 计入 Google；LinkedIn、Microsoft Translator 计入 Microsoft；今日头条计入字节；Naver Labs Europe 计入 NAVER；阿里云、淘天计入阿里巴巴（蚂蚁单列）；A9 计入 Amazon。HLTCOE 和 CLSP 计入约翰斯·霍普金斯大学，ISI 计入 USC。
  • Finance / quant covers trading firms, banks and financial data companies.“金融 / 量化”包括交易公司、银行和金融数据公司。
Estimated amounts估算金额
  • Each sponsor counts at its tier’s price for that conference and year. 2018 uses the 2019 prices, which were the first published; editions before 2018 are not estimated. From 2023 the Supporter tier is no longer listed and counts as $2,000.每家赞助商按当年该会议该等级的价格计。2018 年用 2019 年（最早公开的一份）价格；2018 年以前不估算。2023 年起手册不再列 Supporter 等级，按 $2,000 计。
  • The estimates come within about 10% of the reported income for the five editions where both exist (2022–2023). Sponsors buying several conferences at once get a discount, and some deals include extras, so single editions can be off in either direction.在同时有估算和实际收入的五届（2022–2023）里，估算和实际相差约 10% 以内。同时赞助多个会议的打包有折扣，有些合同还有附加项，所以单届估算可能偏高也可能偏低。
Countries国家 / 地区
  • Two modes, switchable at the top: the sponsoring entity’s headquarters, or its parent company (for example Megagon Labs is in the US, its parent Recruit in Japan). A few small companies could not be located; they stay in the lists but get no country row.两种口径，可在顶部切换：赞助实体总部所在地，或母公司国籍（例如 Megagon Labs 在美国，母公司 Recruit 在日本）。少数小公司无法确定所在地，仍计入名单，但不单列国家。
